# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
A refund can be requested for any order that is eligible for return under the Return Policy, for packages confirmed lost by the carrier, and for cancelled orders that were already charged. Digital gift cards are not eligible for refunds.

## Approval Process
Every refund request is reviewed by a member of the VSR AI support team before money is returned. Requests are reviewed within 1-2 business days. You will be notified in your support conversation when a refund is approved or rejected.

## Refund Method and Timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. After approval, refunds typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days depending on your bank. Store credit is available on request and is issued immediately after approval.

## Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued for items returned without original accessories or packaging, or for items showing signs of use beyond normal inspection. The return shipping fee described in the Return Policy is deducted from non-defective returns.

## Duplicate Requests
Only one active refund request is allowed per order. If a refund request for an order is already pending or approved, a new request cannot be created for the same order.

## Rejected Refunds
If a refund request is rejected, the reason is shared in your support conversation. You may contact support to appeal a rejection within 14 days.
