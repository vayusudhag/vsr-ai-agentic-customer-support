# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage
All electronics sold by the VSR Store, including audio products, wearables, keyboards, mice, chargers, and power banks, include a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. Furniture such as office chairs and standing desks includes a 3-year limited warranty on frames and mechanisms.

## What the Warranty Covers
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Covered issues include battery failure that is not caused by misuse, charging faults, motor failures in standing desks, and broken chair mechanisms.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage beyond the product's rated water resistance, normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratches, or damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications. Kitchen knives and cast-iron cookware are covered against manufacturing defects only.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim
Contact support with your order number, a description of the problem, and photos or video of the defect. If the claim is approved, we will repair or replace the product at no cost. If a replacement is not available, we may issue a refund for the original purchase price.

## Warranty After the Return Window
After the 30-day return window has closed, defective products should be handled as a warranty claim rather than a return.
