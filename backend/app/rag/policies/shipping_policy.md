# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders placed after 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day begin processing the next business day. Orders are not processed on weekends or US federal holidays.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times
Standard Shipping delivers in 3-6 business days after the order ships. Expedited Shipping delivers in 2-3 business days. Overnight Shipping delivers the next business day when the order is placed before 12 PM Eastern Time. Delivery estimates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed.

## Shipping Costs
Standard Shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95 for Standard Shipping. Expedited Shipping costs $14.95 and Overnight Shipping costs $29.95 regardless of order value.

## Carriers and Tracking
We ship with UPS, FedEx, USPS, and DHL. A tracking number is emailed when your order ships and is also visible on the My Orders page. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier website.

## Shipping Destinations
We currently ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and APO/FPO addresses. We do not currently ship internationally.

## Delayed, Lost, or Damaged Packages
If your package has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation. If a package is confirmed lost, we will send a replacement or issue a full refund. Packages that arrive damaged must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage.
