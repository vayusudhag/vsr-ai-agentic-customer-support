# Customer Support FAQ

## How do I contact customer support?
VSR AI Assistant is available 24 hours a day in chat. Human support agents are available Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time and Saturday from 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern Time.

## How do I track my order?
Ask VSR AI Assistant for the status of your order using the order number, which starts with ORD-. You can also open the My Orders page to see the carrier, tracking number, and estimated delivery date.

## Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
The shipping address can be changed only while the order is processing. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Do you price match?
We do not offer price matching with other retailers. If the price of an item drops within 14 days of delivery, contact support and we will refund the difference once per order.

## How is my personal information protected?
Support conversations are visible only to you and authorized VSR support staff. VSR AI Assistant can only access orders that belong to the signed-in account.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Gift wrapping is available for $4.95 per item at checkout. Digital gift cards are delivered by email and cannot be gift wrapped.
