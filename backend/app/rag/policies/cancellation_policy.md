# Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling an Order
Orders can be cancelled at no cost while their status is processing. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled and must be returned after delivery under the Return Policy.

## How to Cancel
To cancel a processing order, contact support or ask VSR AI Assistant with your order number. Cancellations are confirmed by email.

## Cancellation Refunds
If you were charged for an order that is cancelled, the full amount including shipping is refunded to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Cancellations by the VSR Store
We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, if payment cannot be verified, or if there is a pricing error. You will be notified by email and fully refunded.
